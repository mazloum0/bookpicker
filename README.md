# Shelf Talker

Turn a Goodreads review into an Instagram-ready image (post 4:5, story 9:16 or square).

- Type a book title: the title, author and cover fill in from [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org).
- For the sharpest cover, paste the cover image link from the book's Goodreads page.
- Paste your review, pick a look (Spotlight, Paper, Bold) and a typeface (Garamond, Grotesk, Typewriter).
- Save the PNG, copy it, or on a phone use Share to save it to Photos or send it to Instagram.

Everything runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded or stored on a server. Your last card is remembered in your own browser only.

On iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

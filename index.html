Where do I find my review text?

On Goodreads, open My Books, click the book, then open your review. Select the text, copy it and paste it above. Goodreads doesn't let other sites or apps read reviews, so pasting is the one step that isn't automatic. Shorter reviews look best, so choose your favourite two or three sentences.
